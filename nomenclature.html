Inorganic Chemical Nomenclature & Formulas

All 40 compounds here are completely distinct from the final exam so you master the naming rules authentically.

Type I Fixed Charges

1+: Li, Na, K, Rb, Cs, Ag

2+: Be, Mg, Ca, Sr, Ba, Zn

3+: Al

Type II Variable Metals (Roman Numerals)

2+/3+: Fe, Cr, Mn, Co, Ni, Ti, V, Ga

2+/4+: Sn, Pb, Pt | 1+/2+: Cu, Hg | 1+/3+: Au

Oxyacid Suffix Rule

-ate polyatomic ion → -ic acid

-ite polyatomic ion → -ous acid

Binary acid (no oxygen) → hydro-[root]-ic acid
